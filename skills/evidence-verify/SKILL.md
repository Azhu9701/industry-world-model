# Evidence verification

Input: one proposed claim, relation, event, or media asset plus its source.

1. Confirm the URL is retrievable and the publisher, document, and date match the proposal.
2. Capture the smallest sufficient excerpt or stable selector and its hash.
3. Decide `supported`, `contradicted`, `partial`, or `unverifiable`; explain the mismatch precisely.
4. Keep conflicting supported claims side by side and open a review task.
5. Reject circular citations, copied search snippets, unsourced summaries, and model-generated assertions.

Verification means direct support, not plausibility. Do not write to the database outside the review path.
